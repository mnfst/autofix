# Code of conduct

Be kind. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise the code, not the
person who wrote it.

Harassment, personal attacks and bad faith get you removed from the project.
The maintainers decide what counts, and we'd rather act early than argue about
where the line is.

Report anything to conduct@manifest.build. It stays between you and the
maintainers.

This is the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
in short form. Where the two disagree, the Covenant wins.
